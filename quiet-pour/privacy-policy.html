Quiet Pour privacy policy

Nobody Workshop · Effective November 8, 2026

This policy applies to Quiet Pour for Android (com.nobodyworkshop.quietpour). Game progress, settings and reward records stay in private storage on your device. No account is required. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to delete these records.

Ads and choices

Quiet Pour uses Google Mobile Ads for optional rewards and occasional between-level ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform for privacy choices. These services may collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), advertising or app set identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google encrypts SDK traffic in transit. Where available, open Settings in the game to revisit ad privacy options. You may also manage your device advertising identifier in Android settings. Puzzles can be played offline.

Retention and contact

Local game records remain until app storage is cleared. Nobody Workshop does not maintain a player account database. Advertising data is handled under Google's privacy policy and its retention controls. If you choose to email us, we use your address and message to respond and handle your request. Contact service@gamebox.wiki for privacy questions or deletion requests regarding support correspondence.
