Zombie Shooter: Bellwatch Privacy Policy

Nobody Workshop · Android · November 28, 2026

This policy covers the Android game Zombie Shooter: Bellwatch, package com.nobodyworkshop.bellwatch.

Campaign progress and sharing

The game stores your campaign progress, weapon unlocks, best scores and gameplay preferences on your device. Android settings let you clear the game's local storage. If you choose to share an invitation or result card, the game gives the app you select temporary access to the image. The receiving service handles the copy you send under its own policy.

Optional gameplay analytics

Analytics starts off. You can allow or turn off gameplay analytics from the armory. If you allow it, Google Analytics for Firebase processes an app instance identifier, device and app information, sessions, approximate location inferred from network information and mission events, including the mission number, completion and stars. We use these statistics to understand gameplay and improve missions. Turning analytics off stops further collection. Previously collected data is retained according to the Google Analytics property's retention settings. Firebase privacy information.

Advertising

Google AdMob provides optional rewarded ads for weapon unlocks and interstitial ads at eligible mission transitions. Google and its advertising partners may process IP address, approximate location, device identifiers, interactions with ads, and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where applicable, Google's consent form presents advertising choices. You can reopen available advertising privacy options in the game. Google processes advertising data according to its own retention practices. How Google uses data from partner apps.

Reviews and contact

If you choose to review the game, Google Play handles the review. To ask about your data or request deletion of information you have provided directly to Nobody Workshop, contact service@nobodyworkshop.com. Tell us which game and records your request concerns. We use the information you provide to respond, and may retain it as necessary to address the request or comply with legal obligations.
