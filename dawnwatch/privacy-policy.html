Spin Defense: Dawnwatch Privacy Policy

Nobody Workshop · Android · Effective November 25, 2026

This policy applies to Spin Defense: Dawnwatch, package com.nobodyworkshop.dawnwatch. The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over. You can play the campaign offline without creating an account.

Progress and challenge cards

Your coins, squad, campaign checkpoints and challenge results are stored on your device. You can clear the game's storage in Android Settings to erase local progress. Challenge cards contain a game date, rules, score and a reusable challenge code. When you import a picture, the game reads the selected image on your device. When you share, Android gives the receiving app temporary access to the image. You choose the app and recipient. Copies saved to your gallery or sent to someone else can remain after you remove the game.

Optional game analytics

Usage analytics starts turned off. If you enable it in Settings, Google Analytics for Firebase processes app-instance and session identifiers, device and app information, language, approximate region and in-game events such as spins, encounters and challenge completions. We use these to understand how the game performs and where players get stuck. You can withdraw consent in Settings. This stops further game analytics collection and resets local Analytics data. Previously collected data follows the retention settings of our Google Analytics property. Firebase privacy information.

Advertising

Google AdMob provides optional rewarded videos at chapter end and interstitial ads at eligible encounter breaks after inactivity. The base chapter reward is available without watching a video. For ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address (including approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. Advertising choices are separate from game analytics. Where available, use ad privacy options in the game to review or change your choices. Google receives data through its SDK under its own retention practices. How Google uses data.

Feedback, retention and deletion

If you choose to email feedback, your mail app shows an editable draft. We receive the email address, message, and any game details you include when you send it. We use this information to respond and address the issue. Contact Nobody Workshop to ask about personal information or request deletion of a support correspondence. Include enough information to identify the correspondence, without sending passwords. Records may be retained where necessary to resolve the request or meet legal obligations. Clearing local storage does not delete previously sent email or copies held by recipients.
