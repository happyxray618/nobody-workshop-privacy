Real Boxing Champion privacy policy

nobodyworkshop · Updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers the Android game Real Boxing Champion (com.fight.realboxing).

Game progress

The game stores coins, match count, fighter selection and unlocks on your device. You can erase this progress by clearing the game's storage in Android settings.

Advertising and analytics

Versions with Google AdMob may share device and advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information with Google to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain the service. Google may use IP addresses to estimate approximate location. Personalized advertising depends on the privacy choices applicable in your region. You can manage or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Versions with Firebase Analytics may record installation, session and gameplay events, including match starts and outcomes, with app-instance identifiers and device and app information. These events help us understand how the game is used. Optional analytics is subject to your in-game privacy choice. We do not ask you to provide your name or email address to play.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, write to service@gamebox.wiki. Emailing support shares the address and information you send with our support team. Please do not include passwords.
