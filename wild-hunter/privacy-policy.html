Wild Hunter Adventure Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy covers Wild Hunter Adventure for Android (com.nobodyworkshop.wildhunter), developed by Nobody Workshop. Contact: service@gamebox.wiki.

Local game data

Progress, unlocked items, coins, settings, review-request timing and relevant privacy choices are stored on your device. No account or login is required. We do not operate a server that receives your saved game. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local saves, subject to device backup and restore settings.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). A banner may appear on the main menu and interstitial ads may appear when leaving a completed or failed mission. Rewarded videos are optional: the offer explains the coin reward before you choose to watch. Declining does not prevent ordinary gameplay.

Google's advertising SDK collects and shares IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or other device identifiers, interactions with the app and advertisements, and diagnostic and performance information. Google and its partners process this information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, and prevention of fraud and abuse. Non-personalized ads can still involve this processing. The game does not request precise device location, contacts, camera, microphone or personal files for advertising.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Privacy choices

Where applicable, Google's consent interface is presented before advertising requests are permitted. When required by UMP, a Privacy choices button on the main menu lets you revisit your choices. You can also use Android's advertising privacy settings. You may continue ordinary gameplay when ads are unavailable or when you decline optional choices; rewarded-offer availability may be affected.

Ratings, analytics and purchases

The game may request an optional Google Play review after gameplay and provides a link to its Play listing. Google controls whether its review interface appears. Ratings are never rewarded, and we do not receive your chosen rating from the review API. Submitted reviews are handled under Google Play's policies. Separate Unity Analytics and Unity Ads are disabled. In-app purchases are not enabled. Google's advertising measurement and diagnostic processing still applies.

Support and deletion

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive information. Email service@gamebox.wiki to request deletion of information you provided directly to us, subject to applicable retention obligations. Clear app storage in Android settings to remove local saves. Google manages data processed by its services and provides its own privacy controls.

Children and local requirements

Advertising availability, privacy requirements and controls vary by region and user circumstances. If a parent or guardian believes a child sent personal information directly to Nobody Workshop, contact us so we can investigate and respond.

Security and international processing

Local saves depend on your device's security. Third-party providers may process information in other countries under their own policies. No system can guarantee absolute security. This policy does not control external websites you choose to open.

Changes

We may revise this policy as the game or its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required.
