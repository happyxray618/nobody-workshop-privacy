WILD HUNTER ADVENTURE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy describes Wild Hunter Adventure for Android (package com.nobodyworkshop.wildhunter), developed by Nobody Workshop. Contact service@gamebox.wiki with privacy questions.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, unlocked items, coins, settings, review-request timing, and relevant privacy choices locally. No game account or login is required. We do not operate a server that receives your saved game. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local saves, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Advertising and third-party services

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). A banner may appear on the main menu. Interstitial ads may appear when leaving a completed or failed mission. Rewarded videos are optional: the offer explains the coin reward before you choose to watch, and declining does not prevent ordinary gameplay.

Google's advertising SDK collects and shares IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or other device identifiers, interactions with the app and advertisements, and diagnostic and performance information. Google and its partners process this information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, and prevention of fraud and abuse. Non-personalized ads can still involve this processing. The game does not request precise device location, contacts, camera, microphone, or personal files for advertising.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Your privacy choices

Where applicable, Google's consent interface is presented before advertising requests are permitted. When required by UMP, a Privacy choices button on the main menu lets you revisit your choices. You can also use Android's advertising privacy settings. You may continue ordinary gameplay when ads are unavailable or when you decline optional choices; availability of rewarded offers may be affected.

Ratings, analytics, and purchases

The game may request an optional Google Play review after you have experienced gameplay, and provides a direct link to its Play listing. Google controls whether its review interface appears. No reward is offered for ratings, and we do not receive your chosen rating from the review API. Reviews you submit are handled by Google Play under its policies. Separate Unity Analytics and Unity Ads are disabled. In-app purchases are not enabled. Google's advertising measurement and diagnostic processing still applies.

Support and deletion requests

If you email us, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive information. Email service@gamebox.wiki to request deletion of information you provided directly to us, subject to applicable retention obligations. To remove local game saves, clear app storage in Android settings. Google manages information processed through its own services and provides its own privacy controls.

Children and local requirements

Advertising availability, privacy requirements, and controls vary by region and user circumstances. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information directly to Nobody Workshop, contact us so we can investigate and respond.

Security and international processing

Local saves depend on your device's security. Third-party service providers may process information in other countries under their own policies. No system can guarantee absolute security. This policy does not control external websites you choose to open.

Changes

We may revise this policy when the game or its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required.
