Bad Luck Express — Privacy Policy

Nobody Workshop operates Bad Luck Express, version 1.1.0. Last reviewed: 26 October 2026. This notice describes the current version without advertising or analytics SDKs.

No account is required. We do not collect or receive personal information or gameplay records through this app. HP, rest times, draw history, relay chain identifiers and receive results stay in private storage on your device. Language and welcome preferences are stored locally. Android cloud backup and app-data device transfer are excluded.

The current unclaimed envelope preview is also saved locally, so it can be restored after the app process ends. Leaving the incoming screen explicitly clears that pending preview. Restoring it never receives a card or changes HP.

Sharing is optional. A relay code or link contains a protocol version, random relay chain ID, official card ID, handoff number and error-detection checksum. It contains no name, email, contact list, location or device advertising identifier. The checksum is not a secure signature. Receiving requires your explicit action in the Android app; a website preview cannot change HP.

When you choose Share, Android hands the chosen text or retirement image to the app you select. That app and your recipients may retain or forward it under their own policies. Nobody Workshop does not operate a relay server or track recipients. We cannot delete copies kept by other people or apps.

No advertising SDK, analytics SDK, Firebase, account service or business API is included. There are no ad identifiers or consent prompts in this version. If advertising is added later, its actual data practices and any required consent controls must be disclosed before release; no advertising data is currently processed by an ad SDK here.

You can remove local progress using Android Settings → Apps → Bad Luck Express → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This permanently removes your pet, draws and local duplicate protection. Browser language preference can be cleared through browser site-data settings. Copies already shared through other apps are separate.

The public privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and ordinary request, browser and device information for hosting and security under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Nobody Workshop adds no website analytics or advertising trackers and does not set a separate hosting-log retention period. The companion preview stores a language preference locally in the browser. (localStorage)

Cards are fictional entertainment, not fortune prediction or medical care. We do not collect personal profiles or freeform chat. If you email support, we receive your email address and the message you choose to send, use them to answer your request, and retain them only as needed to handle it or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting us.
