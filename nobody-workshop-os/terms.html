Nobody Workshop OS Usage Information

Updated November 15, 2026.

Nobody Workshop OS is an owner-operated local reporting tool. Connect only accounts and resources you are authorized to access. The current Google reporting connectors use source-specific read-only/reporting permissions.

Reports may be delayed, incomplete or revised by their official sources. AdMob revenue is estimated. A missing report row is not evidence of zero activity, and a displayed report does not guarantee future revenue or advertising performance.

The owner controls local data, backups, exports, scheduled jobs and account revocation. This OAuth reporting configuration does not authorize advertising-budget changes, campaign launches or Google Play publication. Those actions require separate explicit owner authorization.

For support, contact service@gamebox.wiki. See the privacy policy for Google-data use, storage, retention and deletion.
