Nobody Workshop OS

A local operations dashboard for Nobody Workshop's own Google Play apps and games.

Purpose

The studio owner connects existing Google accounts to inspect app identity, installation reports, estimated advertising revenue, app quality and aggregate analytics. Reports are stored on the owner's Windows computer and shown in a dashboard bound to 127.0.0.1. This is an owner-operated tool, not a public account-management service.

Google connections

  • Google Play reports: read statistics from the owner's authorized Cloud Storage reporting bucket.
  • Android Vitals: read app identity and quality reports through the Google Play Developer Reporting API.
  • AdMob: read app identity and estimated revenue reports.
  • Firebase: read project and Android app metadata.
  • Google Analytics: read property, data-stream and aggregate reporting data.

Each connector requests its existing, source-specific read-only/reporting permission. This reporting setup does not request Google Ads campaign access and does not create campaigns, change budgets or submit Google Play releases. Separately approved advertising features are outside this reporting setup.

Account control

Only connect an account you own or are authorized to operate. You can revoke the tool's access in Google Account connections at any time. Revoking access stops future reads; it does not automatically erase previously saved local reports.
