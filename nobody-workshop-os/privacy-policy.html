Nobody Workshop OS Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026. Operator: Nobody Workshop.

Scope and data accessed

This policy covers Nobody Workshop OS, the studio owner's local operations dashboard. It does not replace privacy policies for the studio's consumer apps or games.

With the account owner's authorization, the tool reads Google Play package identities and installation statistics, Android Vitals quality reports, AdMob app identities and estimated revenue, Firebase project/app metadata, and Google Analytics properties, data streams and aggregate activity reports. The current analytics importer reads aggregate active-user, session and event-count metrics. It does not request Gmail, contacts, Drive documents, passwords, payment-card details or user message contents.

Use of Google data

Data is used to identify the owner's apps, show operating results, validate source identity and completeness, compare report periods and investigate missing or failed reads. Missing data is kept distinct from an explicit zero. Google data is not sold, used for advertising targeting or used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Nobody Workshop OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Reports, raw report revisions, source identifiers and audit history are stored in local databases and files on the operator's computer. OAuth credentials are kept in the Windows credential store; approved credential-file references, where used, remain outside the source repository. Credentials are not intended for publication, chat, report exports or logs. Google API requests use HTTPS. The local dashboard binds to 127.0.0.1 by default.

Sharing and access

The tool does not operate a central server that collects connected account reports. The operator can deliberately export or share selected reports for authorized analysis, including human-directed assistance. Such sharing is under the operator's control; OAuth credentials must not be shared. Access by other people to the computer, local backups or exported files is controlled by the operator.

Retention and deletion

Local reports, original revisions and audit evidence are retained until the operator removes them. To stop new access, revoke Nobody Workshop OS in Google Account connections and stop scheduled jobs. To erase stored data, remove the tool's local reports/databases, relevant backups/exports and its source-specific Windows credentials. Revocation alone does not delete local history. The operator can contact us for deletion guidance.

This public website

These static information pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or application login. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process hosting request information under its privacy statement. Private reports and credentials are not uploaded to this website.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, email service@gamebox.wiki. Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
