PRIVACY POLICY

Business Days Calculator

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy applies to Business Days & Date Calculator, published as Business Days Calculator by Nobody Workshop, package com.nobodyworkshop.businessdays.

Your calculations and local storage

Date inputs, five recent calculations, working weekdays, excluded dates and labels, date format and appearance settings stay in private storage on your device. Calculations work offline, without an account. We do not upload your calculation dates or labels to a developer server. The batch unlock lasts for the current app process; the expiry of a rewarded 30-minute period without banner ads is saved locally and survives an app restart.

Advertising and third parties

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for adaptive banners on Calculate and History, and optional rewarded ads for batch calculations or 30 minutes without banners. Core single calculations and result explanations do not require watching an ad. Google may collect and share IP addresses (which may infer approximate location), device identifiers including the advertising ID where available, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, fraud prevention and analytics. This information is processed by Google and its advertising partners; hiding banners does not turn off an optional rewarded ad or erase prior advertising data.

We do not use a developer analytics service, Firebase, cloud synchronization or account system. Please read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Consent and choices

Google's User Messaging Platform provides applicable consent choices. Where required, use Advertising privacy in Settings to review those choices. Ad requests begin only when the consent SDK permits them. You can manage the advertising ID and ad personalization in your Android/Google settings. Declining a choice, lack of network connectivity or an ad failure does not prevent basic calculations. Rewarded features are granted only after the ad SDK reports the reward.

Permissions

Advertising uses internet access, network state and the advertising ID permission. The app does not request calendar, contacts, precise location, camera or broad storage access. Copying a result places the selected result on your clipboard; an app where you paste it can receive that result.

Deletion and retention

Use Clear history, edit/delete excluded dates, or clear app storage/uninstall to remove local records and rewarded expiry. App data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer. We do not hold developer-side accounts or calculation records. Google's retention and deletion practices are governed by its own policies and controls.

External links

Opening a website or contacting support is processed by your browser/email service. This policy is hosted by GitHub Pages, subject to GitHub's privacy statement. Opening it does not send your calculations.

Children

This utility is designed for adults and is not directed at children.

Policy changes

We update this policy when data practices change.
