PRIVACY POLICY

Business Days Calculator

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy applies to Business Days & Date Calculator, published as Business Days Calculator by Nobody Workshop, package com.nobodyworkshop.businessdays. It describes our current production release, which has no advertising or analytics SDK.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your date inputs, five recent calculations, selected working weekdays, manually excluded dates and labels, date format and appearance settings in the app's private local storage. Calculations run on your device, including when offline. You do not need an account.

Collection and sharing

We do not receive or upload your calculations, saved dates, labels, history or settings. The current production app has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, server API or cloud synchronization. We do not sell or share your app data.

Permissions

The current production app does not request internet, calendar, contacts, location, camera or storage access. Copying a result places only the result you choose on your device clipboard; another app you use to paste that result may then receive it.

Your choices and deletion

Use Clear history to remove recent calculations. In Settings you can edit or delete excluded dates and change your working week and preferences. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes all its local records and settings. App data is not included in Android backup or device transfer. There is no developer-held account or data record to delete.

External links

If you open a website link or contact support, your browser or email provider processes the information you provide according to its own policies. This privacy website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process website visitor information under its privacy statement. Opening this page does not upload your app calculations.

Children

This utility is designed for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Policy changes

If the app's data handling changes, we will update this policy and its effective date. An advertising-enabled release would require an updated policy and applicable store disclosures before distribution.

Contact

Developer: Nobody Workshop. Support and privacy questions: service@gamebox.wiki. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your message.
