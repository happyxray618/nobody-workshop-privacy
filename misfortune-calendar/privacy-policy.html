Misfortune Calendar Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Misfortune Calendar is an entertainment app by Nobody Workshop. For entertainment purposes only. Its playful scores and stories do not predict real events or provide professional advice.

1. Local reports, preferences and storage

No account is required. We operate no app backend and do not use Firebase or publisher analytics. A random anonymous installation seed, daily report dates and results, chosen interface language and optional rewarded-ad unlock records remain in private storage on your device. These records are not uploaded to Nobody Workshop. Cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled in the app configuration. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local records; reinstalling may create a different anonymous identity.

2. Optional advertising and consent

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The only advertising placement is a rewarded ad that you may choose to watch for an extra daily interpretation, up to three per date subject to a conservative local time-based limit. Today's basic report and tomorrow's forecast remain free and do not require watching ads. We do not show banner, interstitial or app-open ads.

Google advertising services may collect and share IP address (including approximate location inferred from IP), advertising and other device identifiers, device/app information, advertising interactions, diagnostics and performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. UMP processes consent choices and relevant request/device information. Processing depends on service configuration, your location and applicable choices. Advertising requests are gated by UMP's consent eligibility. The app does not send your local report seed or personal report records as ad targeting information.

Open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review available choices. You may also manage advertising privacy in Android settings. Available messages vary by region and configured Google services. Please see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. Google may process data internationally, and its retention/deletion rules are governed by those services.

3. Images, sharing and reviews

Share cards are rendered locally. Saving a card writes your requested PNG to Pictures/Misfortune Calendar through Android MediaStore. Sharing sends the selected card via a temporary read-only FileProvider grant to the app you choose; that service then applies its own privacy policy. Cards show a date, playful score, humorous text and Nobody Workshop branding, not your local random seed or advertising identifier. Temporary share cache files may remain for up to seven days and are cleaned up on later shares. Saved gallery copies remain until you delete them.

Our optional review link opens Google Play. Reviews and any public profile information you choose to publish are processed by Google Play. No rating is required or rewarded, and we do not filter users by whether feedback is positive.

4. Permissions

Supported Android versions are 10 and above. No contacts, precise-location, camera, microphone, storage-read or media-read runtime permission is requested. Internet and network-state access support ads/consent and user-requested external links. Google advertising libraries declare advertising ID, AdServices, wake-lock and foreground-service capabilities; the app itself starts no background foreground-service workflow.

5. Support, security and data rights

If you contact service@gamebox.wiki, we receive your address and the information you choose to send, solely to respond and handle legitimate support, legal or security needs. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless necessary. Support messages are kept only as needed for those purposes. We do not sell local report data. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system guarantees absolute security. Clear app storage to remove local app records and delete gallery exports separately. For support-information deletion or applicable privacy-rights requests, email us. Deleting local data does not automatically remove information independently held by Google or a sharing recipient.

6. Audience and changes

The app is intended for adults seeking gentle everyday humor and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child supplied personal data through support, please contact us. We will update this policy as functionality or data practices change and revise the date above.
