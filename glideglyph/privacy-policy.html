Glideglyph Privacy Policy

Updated November 8, 2026. This policy covers Arrow Puzzle: Glideglyph for Android, package com.nobodyworkshop.glideglyph.

Your game progress

Glideglyph saves your campaign level, coins, boosters, completion count, review invitation status, and sound and display preferences on your device. You can play without creating an account. Clearing the app's storage removes the local save.

Sharing a challenge

When you tap Share result, Glideglyph passes the challenge identifier, puzzle name, and score to the Android share sheet. You choose whether to send it and which app receives it. The receiving app handles the text under its own privacy policy.

Age selection and children

Before play, choose an age range or select Prefer not to say. The game stores only an adult or restricted-play setting on the device. It does not upload your answer or ask for a birth date. For players under 18 and players who decline to answer, the game does not initialize the advertising or ad-consent service, and the daily sprint offers its single revive without an ad.

Advertising and privacy choices

For players who select 18 or older, Glideglyph uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, rewarded video and app open ads. Rewarded videos are optional. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, device identifiers such as the app set ID, interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. An IP address can indicate approximate location. The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request applicable consent and determine whether ads may be requested. Where Google requires a privacy options entry point, open game settings and tap Ad privacy to revisit your choices.

Read Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures, Google advertising information, and Google's privacy policy for information about processing, retention, and controls.

Reviews and support

The review invitation can open Google Play. Google handles reviews under its own policies. If you email Nobody Workshop, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. For privacy questions or a request about support correspondence, contact service@gamebox.wiki.
