Privacy Policy

Optional usage statistics in version 1.0.2

You can enable Firebase Analytics using the usage statistics control in the app. It is off by default. When enabled, Google processes an app-instance identifier, device information, approximate location derived from the connection, session activity and events such as starting and completing levels to help Nobody Workshop understand how people use the app. You can turn it off at any time using the same control. These events exclude names, email addresses, messages and user-entered content.

Updated November 6, 2026. This policy applies to the advertising-enabled release of Viperwake: Jet Shooter.

Viperwake: Jet Shooter — Privacy Policy
Developer: Nobody Workshop
Contact: service@gamebox.wiki

This policy describes the Android game Viperwake: Jet Shooter (com.nobodyworkshop.viperwake).

Game data: The game stores progress, stars, upgrades, unlocked content, settings and privacy choices on your device. A game account is not required and Nobody Workshop does not provide a cloud save for this version. You can clear local data in Android settings. Uninstalling or clearing storage may remove your progress. Android backup, restore and device transfer may retain or restore copies depending on your settings.

Advertising: The game uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to offer banner, interstitial, optional rewarded and app-open ads. Google and its partners may process IP addresses, approximate location, advertising and other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and provide services. Non-personalized or limited ads can also involve processing. Google and participating partners may process data outside your country. Google explains its practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Privacy choices: The game asks your age group and keeps ads off when age is unknown or under 18. Where required, Google's consent message offers choices, including refusal and an option to manage settings. You can return to Privacy & ads from the game menu and open Ad privacy choices when available, or disable ads in the game. Disabling ads stops future ad requests in this game; it does not erase data already processed by Google or partners. You may also use Android's advertising privacy controls. Optional GameAnalytics gameplay analytics is not enabled in this version.

Support: If you email service@gamebox.wiki, we receive your address and any information you choose to send, and use it to respond and investigate. Please do not send passwords, keys, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. We retain support correspondence only as needed for the request and applicable legal, security or dispute obligations, and handle requests for access, correction or deletion through this address. Ask us about Viperwake and provide only information reasonably needed to locate your correspondence. Local game data is controlled on your device; Google data is also subject to Google's own privacy and deletion controls.

Audience and security: The game is intended for adults aged 18 and older. The age selection is self-declared. The game does not require camera, microphone, contacts or precise GPS permission to play. Protect your device and backups; no system offers absolute security. We may update this policy when practices change. Contact Nobody Workshop at service@gamebox.wiki with questions or privacy requests.
