Project Later Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Project Later is provided by Nobody Workshop. This policy explains how information is used when you sign in, connect with friends, arrange plans, use notifications, or use optional subscriptions and advertising.

Information we process

  • Account and profile: Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication process your Google account identity, which can include your name, email address and profile picture. Project Later stores your Firebase user identifier, display name, chosen username, profile picture URL and account timestamps.
  • Friend and planning activity: friend requests and relationships, your private Active Circle and archive selections, blocks, Signals and their activities and audience, Pings, proposed times, Plan membership and status, and completion responses.
  • Notifications and operation: notification preferences, device registration and FCM delivery tokens, notification delivery and deduplication records, and technical or security logs needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Purchases and rewards: subscription product and base plan, verified purchase state and expiry, purchase-token hashes and account binding, entitlement and capacity records, and reward verification sessions and transaction identifiers. Purchase tokens are sent to our verification service and Google Play for verification. Project Later does not receive your payment card details; Google Play handles payment.
  • Advertising: Google Mobile Ads and its services may process advertising or device identifiers, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics. Reward verification also processes a random session identifier and a hashed account identifier to associate a verified reward with your account. We request consent where required before requesting ads.

Why we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, display profiles, manage friend relationships and visibility, show authorized Signals and Plans, deliver requested notifications, verify subscriptions and optional rewards, synchronize your account across devices, prevent abuse and duplicate actions, and provide support. Project Later does not request precise location access and does not provide location sharing.

Who receives information

Friends and Plan participants can see profile and activity information shared with them according to the app's friendship, audience, membership and Block controls. Your Active Circle and archive lists are private account management information. Blocking limits new interactions; it does not erase another participant's existing Plan history.

We use Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging to run the service, Google Play Billing to manage purchases, and Google AdMob to provide advertising. These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Information may be processed on infrastructure outside your country. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service and its users.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Advertising choices

Banner ads are limited to Friends management pages for eligible accounts. A valid Plus entitlement removes those banners. Rewarded ads are optional and offer at most one permanent extra Active Circle slot per account, after server verification. Ad availability is not guaranteed. You can manage applicable advertising preferences through Google and your device settings; consent controls apply where required.

Storage, retention and deletion

The app caches account information and trusted entitlement state locally to support operation and recovery. Cloud account and relationship records are retained to provide the service. A Signal becoming hidden at expiry, archiving a friend, or a subscription expiring does not by itself delete account or Plan history. We retain information as necessary for the service and for legitimate security, fraud prevention and legal purposes.

To request access, correction, account deletion or deletion of associated personal data, contact service@gamebox.wiki from an address associated with your account and identify Project Later. Do not send passwords or payment card details. We may need to verify your identity. Some information may be retained where required for legal obligations, security or transaction integrity. Records involving other participants may need to be retained with identifying information removed where appropriate. Uninstalling the app does not itself delete your cloud account or cancel a Google Play subscription; manage subscriptions separately in Google Play.

Children and account requirements

Use of the service must comply with applicable age and account requirements. If you believe personal information has been provided without required parental authorization, contact us to request review and removal.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest revision. For privacy questions, contact Nobody Workshop at service@gamebox.wiki.
