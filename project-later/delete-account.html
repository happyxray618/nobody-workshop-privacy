Delete your Freeyo account

Freeyo is provided by Nobody Workshop. You can request deletion without reinstalling the app or signing in to this website.

Email an account deletion request

If the link does not open an email app, write to service@gamebox.wiki with the subject Freeyo account deletion request. Send from the email address used for your Freeyo Google sign-in and include your Freeyo username. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens or payment card details.

What happens next

Support verifies ownership before processing the request and confirms the outcome and applicable retention exceptions. Opening an email draft does not submit a request; you must send it. This is a support-assisted process, not instant automated deletion. If you have lost access to the original account email, explain that to support so ownership can be reviewed.

Requested deletion scope

The request covers the Freeyo authentication account and profile, username mapping, friend relationships, Active Circle records, Signals and their audience references, Pings, Plans and Plan membership, device notification tokens and preferences, and account entitlement associations. Support must assess shared records involving other people and explain any information that needs to remain or be de-identified. Expiry, archiving and signing out are not account deletion.

Records that may need to remain

Limited purchase-verification, anti-fraud and abuse/security records may be retained where necessary for transaction integrity, legal obligations or resolving disputes. The current service stores purchase-token ownership bindings and hashed billing/reward events; these are not automatically erased when an entitlement expires. Google Play maintains its own transaction history. Provider logs and backups may remain under their retention processes. We do not promise that all records disappear immediately. Ask support for the handling and retention applicable to your request.

Subscriptions are separate

Uninstalling Freeyo or requesting account deletion does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop renewal, use Google Play subscriptions. You can submit a deletion request even if you still need help managing a subscription; support can explain the separate steps.

In the updated app, use Me → Delete account → Open email draft. You can always use the email address above, including from older versions.
