Freeyo child safety and community standards

Freeyo is provided by Nobody Workshop and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It helps existing friends share availability and make plans; it does not offer public feeds, stranger discovery or a dating service. These limits do not excuse abuse or remove our child safety responsibilities.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation, sexual solicitation of minors, trafficking and conduct that facilitates child abuse or exploitation are prohibited. Do not use profile names, friend requests, Signals, Pings or Plans to facilitate or promote such conduct. Do not impersonate another person, harass, threaten, spam, or bypass another person's Block controls.

Report a concern

In the updated app, use Friends → Report safety concern, select the reason and open the private report email. A report is sent only when you send that email. You may instead write directly to service@gamebox.wiki with subject Freeyo child safety report. Include relevant Freeyo usernames and a description. Do not send illegal imagery, passwords, tokens or payment information. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Protect your account

Friends controls provide Block, Unblock and Remove friend in the updated app. Block limits new interactions and notifications under the service's privacy controls; existing shared Plan history is not automatically erased. Removing a friend ends the friendship. Neither action is a substitute for reporting a serious concern.

Review and enforcement

Nobody Workshop reviews reports, assesses relevant available service records and may restrict or disable violating accounts, remove prohibited account content, or take other appropriate action. Confirmed CSAM and child exploitation are handled under applicable laws, including reporting to NCMEC or the relevant regional authority as applicable. Necessary evidence may be preserved securely for legal handling. We do not promise round-the-clock monitoring or a specific response time. Reporters can contact the same address for follow-up or to request review of an enforcement decision.

Child safety contact

Nobody Workshop's child safety and abuse contact is service@gamebox.wiki. This contact handles the associated child safety inquiries and escalation process.
