How it works

From a shift to paid hours

For example: 22:00 to 06:00 is an eight-hour overnight shift. Subtract a 30-minute unpaid break to get 7 hours 30 minutes of paid time. Pay depends on the hourly rate and per-shift overtime settings you enter.

  1. Enter the shift start and end times, unpaid break and hourly rate.
  2. Choose an overtime threshold and multiplier if you need them. Check paid hours, regular pay, overtime pay and the estimated total.
  3. Save the shift on your device and review weekly totals. Shifts are grouped by their start date.
A few things to know

Before you give it a try.

Can it calculate work hours across midnight?

Yes. An end time earlier than the start time is treated as the next day. Unpaid breaks are subtracted from the shift.

Does it calculate weekly overtime automatically?

No. The threshold and multiplier you choose apply to each shift separately. The app does not automatically apply local labor laws. Pay is an estimate before taxes and deductions.

Can I use it offline without an account?

Yes. Calculations and saved work records work offline with no registration. Advertising services need internet.

Are my saved shifts synced or exported?

No. Work records stay on this device, with no cloud sync or export. Uninstalling removes local records.
