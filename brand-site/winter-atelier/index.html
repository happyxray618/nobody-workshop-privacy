How it works

Match three tiles, then make the cabin your own

Looking for a triple tile matching puzzle with a cozy setting? The matching board is the core game: clear three identical tiles at a time, keep space in the tray, and reveal tiles underneath. Cabin decoration follows your puzzle progress.

  1. Tap a tile to move it from the board into the holding tray.
  2. Collect three identical tiles in the tray to clear them. Plan your next match before the seven slots fill.
  3. Keep matching until the board is empty. Progress through puzzle levels to unlock cabin decorations; use Undo, Hint or Shuffle when you need help.
