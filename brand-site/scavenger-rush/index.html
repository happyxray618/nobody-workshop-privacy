Hidden Objects: Scavenger Rush

Check your list, zoom in on a bustling illustrated map, and tap the objects you spot. Take your time in Explore or see how many combos you can chain in a short round of Rush.

Three ways to play

  1. Explore at your own pace. Pan and zoom across themed maps, collect the items on your list, and pick up where you left off.
  2. Find 10 objects in Rush. Chain correct finds to build a combo and trigger Fever. Aim for three stars or beat your personal best.
  3. Try Daily Practice. Replay a local challenge with fixed rules and see if you can improve your score.

Frequently asked questions

Can I continue my Explore hunt later?

Yes. You can leave an Explore hunt and return to it later.

Can I replay Daily Practice?

Yes. Replay the local daily challenge to improve your personal best.

Can I tone down the visual effects?

Yes. Turn on Reduced Motion in the settings for gentler effects.
