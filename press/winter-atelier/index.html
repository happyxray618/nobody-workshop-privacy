Winter Atelier: Tile Match

Match three tiles, collect winter keepsakes, and decorate a cabin.

Open the released Android game on Google Play

Facts

Developer
NobodyWorkshop, independent solo developer
Platform and status
Android; publicly released on Google Play. Latest update displayed: 12 November 2026.
Gameplay
Tap tiles into a seven-slot holding tray. Three identical tiles clear from the tray. Clear layered boards, build consecutive-match combos, and use Undo, Hint or Shuffle tools when needed.
Content and progression
3,000 puzzle levels, winter keepsakes, cabin decorations, seasonal visual themes and three daily goals.
Offline and account
Core puzzle play and progression work offline. No account is required for regular gameplay. Advertising and external services have separate network behaviour.
