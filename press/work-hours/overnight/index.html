What this browser tool does

It calculates clock-time hours for one shift under 24 hours. Equal start and end times mean zero hours, not a full day. Breaks must be whole minutes from 0 to 720 and shorter than a nonzero shift. It does not handle dates, daylight-saving changes, multiple-day shifts, wages or labour-law entitlements.
