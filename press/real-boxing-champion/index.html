Real Boxing Champion

A short 3D boxing bout: manage stamina, find an opening, and win by knockout or points.

What the published game offers

  • 45-second matches against a computer-controlled boxer in a 3D ring.
  • Jabs, straight punches and uppercuts, with stamina to manage.
  • Win early by knockout, or reach a points decision at the end.
  • The results screen reports both fighters' punches, hits and accuracy.
  • Earn coins from wins or accumulate matches to unlock more fighters.
