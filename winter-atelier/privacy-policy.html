WINTER ATELIER: TILE MATCH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Nobody Workshop operates Winter Atelier: Tile Match for Android, package com.nobodyworkshop.winteratelier. This policy covers the current ads-only version, including its internal test release. Contact service@gamebox.wiki for privacy questions.

Your game and local information

No game account, login or in-app purchase is required or offered in this version. Core puzzles work offline. Progress, coins, tool counts, collections, cabin decorations, daily goals, settings and advertising reward receipts are stored on your device. Reward receipts and claim limits prevent duplicate rewards and help recover an earned reward after interruption. We do not operate a game-save upload service for this version. Device backup and restore settings may separately affect local data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove local progress.

Optional gameplay analytics

Optional gameplay analytics is off by default. If you choose Allow in the game's Settings, Google Analytics for Firebase can process app-instance identifiers, device and application information, sessions and gameplay interactions, such as starting, completing or retrying a level, completing daily goals and receiving an advertising reward. Events can include a level number and reward amount. We use these events to understand game usage and improve reliability and gameplay. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, purchase credentials or message contents as gameplay event parameters. Unity Analytics is disabled.

You can return to Settings and choose Turn off to stop future optional gameplay analytics. Closing the choice without allowing it does not opt you in. Turning collection off does not automatically erase previously collected records. Google Analytics retention and deletion settings govern records already collected; see the support section below for requests and limitations.

Advertising

The game includes player-initiated rewarded offers and occasional interstitial advertisements at level breaks. Declining an optional reward offer or an unavailable ad does not prevent ordinary offline puzzles. The current internal test build uses Google's official test advertisements; production advertising has not been enabled. This does not mean the advertising SDK makes no network requests or processes no data.

Google Mobile Ads can collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or other device identifiers, app and advertising interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google and its partners use these for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Non-personalized or test advertisements can still involve data processing. The game does not request precise device location, contacts, camera, microphone or personal files for advertising. Optional analytics settings do not themselves switch off all advertising SDK processing. Production advertising requires applicable consent and regional controls to be validated before it is enabled.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google advertising information, Mobile Ads data disclosures, and Firebase privacy information. Google SDK service communications use encrypted transport; this is not a promise of absolute security.

Reviews

After completing level three, the game may request a Google Play review. Google controls whether its review card appears. Reviewing is optional, gives no reward and does not change your progress. We do not receive the rating you select through the review API. Reviews you submit are handled by Google Play.

Support, retention and deletion

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer and investigate your request, and retain them as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Local game information remains until removed through device/app-storage controls, subject to backup settings. SDK data is handled according to the relevant service configuration and Google's policies; we do not promise an unverified fixed retention period. Email service@gamebox.wiki to request access to or deletion of information you provided directly to Nobody Workshop, or for help with analytics-related requests. We may need enough non-sensitive information to locate a record and may be unable to associate a pseudonymous app-instance record with your email identity. Google provides its own privacy and advertising-identifier controls. No account-deletion workflow is required to play because this version has no game account.

Age, security and international processing

The game is intended for people aged 13 and older, including teenagers and adults; it is not directed to children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can investigate. Applicable consent requirements can vary by region and age. Local data depends on your device's security; service providers may process data outside your country under their applicable safeguards and policies. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date above identifies the revision. Material changes will be communicated where required. We will update the policy and disclosures before enabling a materially different production advertising or data-processing configuration.
