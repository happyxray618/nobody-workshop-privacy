BREACH BLITZ: AUTO SHOOTER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy describes how Nobody Workshop's Android game Breach Blitz: Auto Shooter (package com.nobodyworkshop.breachblitz) handles information. Contact us at service@gamebox.wiki with privacy questions.

Game information stored on your device

The game stores progress, unlocked items, currency balances, settings, and relevant privacy choices on your device. No game account or login is required. This release does not upload your saved game to a Nobody Workshop server. Clearing the game's app storage or uninstalling it removes its local saved data; Android backup or device restore behavior may depend on your device settings.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform) to provide advertising and manage applicable privacy choices. Rewarded ads are optional: an offer explains the game reward before you choose to watch. Other ads may appear between completed gameplay activities.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share information such as IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or other device identifiers, interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic and performance information. Google and its advertising partners use this information for delivering and measuring advertising, analytics, and preventing fraud and abuse. Non-personalized advertising can still involve processing information for these purposes. The game does not request precise device location, contacts, microphone, camera, or your personal files for advertising.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures for details about Google's practices, retention, and controls.

Privacy choices

Where applicable, Google's consent interface is shown before advertising requests are allowed. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, the main menu displays an AD PRIVACY button that lets you revisit those choices. You can also use your Android device's advertising privacy settings. Declining optional advertising choices does not require you to stop playing; the availability of advertising or rewarded offers may be affected.

Analytics and purchases

Separate Unity Analytics collection is disabled in this release. In-app purchases are not enabled in this release. Google's advertising measurement and diagnostic processing described above still applies when its advertising services are used.

Information you send to support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and problem description. We use it to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. Contact us to request deletion of information you have provided directly to us, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Children and local requirements

Advertising eligibility, privacy notices, and available controls can vary by country and user circumstances. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information directly to Nobody Workshop, please contact us so we can investigate and respond appropriately.

Security, third parties, and international processing

Local game data remains subject to your device's security. Google and other service operators process information under their own policies, including processing in other countries. No system can guarantee absolute security. This policy does not control the practices of third-party websites opened from the game or this page.

Updates

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required.
