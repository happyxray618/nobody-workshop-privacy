Payday Survival — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026. Developer: Nobody Workshop. Package: com.nobodyworkshop.salarydisappearing. Contact: service@gamebox.wiki.

Payday Survival was previously called Salary Disappearing / 薪资消失器. This policy covers the same Android application and package; the name change does not create a new account or move your records.

Local financial records

The app stores your salary amounts, currency, payday-cycle dates, expenses, categories, notes, fixed expenses and Salary Autopsy reports on your device. Core tracking works offline. There is no app account, bank connection, custom backend or cloud-sync service. We do not send your financial records to our server or to advertising services. Preferences include theme, onboarding, cycle-specific rewarded access and review timing. Pro ownership is checked with Google Play, not established by a local Pro switch.

Sharing you choose

The V1.2 survival-image feature creates a 1080 × 1920 PNG locally on your device. You can preview it before choosing whether and where to share it. The generated image uses an allowlist of relative survival or early-death day counts, outcome or estimate state, and generic copy. It excludes salary and expense amounts, percentages, absolute dates, categories, notes, transaction and cycle IDs, and advertising or purchase identifiers. This reduces disclosure; it is not a guarantee that a story or surrounding context can never identify you.

The app does not upload the generated image to a developer server. It temporarily stores the image in private cache and, if you choose to share, opens the Android system chooser with temporary read access for the selected receiving app. Old exports are cleaned up on a best-effort basis on a later launch or export when over 24 hours old; this is not an exact deletion timer. A successful local financial reset also clears cached exports. Recipients can save, upload, forward or otherwise process a copy under their own terms and privacy practices, beyond this app's control.

Existing text-report sharing is separate and can include financial amounts and dates. Review the preview and sensitive-data notice before sharing. You control whether to share and which receiving app to select; cancelling the chooser does not post a report.

Optional ads and Google services

Advanced insights may be unlocked with a user-initiated rewarded ad or an optional one-time Pro purchase. Basic salary tracking remains free. No banner, interstitial or app-open ads are implemented. The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for ads and consent. Advertising services may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), advertising/app-set or other applicable device/account identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Ad requests depend on applicable consent and eligibility. The app does not request precise location or send financial notes, amounts or reports to these SDKs. There is no separate app-integrated analytics SDK, but advertising SDK measurement and diagnostics still occur.

Google Play Billing processes purchase information. The app receives product/price, purchase state, token and signature information to verify, acknowledge and restore Pro. It does not receive your payment-card details. Google Play also handles any review you choose to submit. We do not receive the rating through the in-app review API. Google services communicate over the network; the financial core does not require those services.

See Google Privacy Policy, Google Ads SDK data disclosures and Google Play Data safety information. Third-party services control their own retention and deletion policies.

Advertising partners and consent

Where applicable, Google's UMP presents Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. The form identifies the advertising partners and links to their privacy policies, processing purposes, storage and other declared details. Depending on your choices and the applicable legal basis, partners may process device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, advertising interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, personalization, measurement, audience research, service development, security and fraud prevention. The app does not send your financial ledger or reports to these services. A partner listed in the consent interface is not necessarily receiving data on every ad request.

Some partners declare precise-geolocation processing or cross-device/data-source matching in their general advertising declarations. The app itself does not request Android precise, approximate or background location permissions, read GPS coordinates, or supply coordinates to advertising SDKs. The consent form's partner declarations describe those partners' processing scope; they are not a statement that the app reads your GPS position. Review each partner's details and any special-feature choices before consenting.

Not all processing is necessarily based on consent. Some partners may rely on legitimate interests, and technical delivery, security and saving privacy choices can have separate grounds. Use Manage options to review purposes and vendor choices and object to legitimate-interest processing where offered. Refusing personalized-ad consent does not mean that no network requests or technical data processing can occur. We do not require personalized-ad consent to use core salary tracking.

You can return to Settings → advertising privacy options to review or withdraw consent and adjust objections when the UMP form is available. If the form is unavailable, skip optional ads, check connectivity and try again; you can still use core tracking. Withdrawal affects subsequent eligible processing, not records already held by Google or a recipient. Android settings also provide advertising-identifier controls. The applicable form and partner policy links provide further details.

Your choices, retention and deletion

Use Settings to review advertising privacy choices where available, and Android settings to manage advertising identifiers. You can decline optional rewarded ads and continue using basic tracking. Financial records remain until you edit/delete them, reset local data, or remove app storage. Confirmed local Reset clears financial cycles, expenses, categories, fixed obligations, reports and earned rewards and returns to setup. Currency/theme and review-frequency metadata are intentionally retained. To remove all app-local data, clear app storage or uninstall. Reset does not cancel/refund Pro or delete Google-held transactions, ad data, reviews or reports you already shared. Google Play ownership can be restored.

Security and backup

App-configured Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled/excluded. Independent copies, rooted devices or manufacturer tools may behave differently. The app does not claim application-level database encryption or forensic secure erasure. Protect your device. Google Ads data is encrypted in transit according to Google's SDK documentation; local storage security depends on Android and your device configuration.

Contact and changes

For questions or privacy requests contact service@gamebox.wiki. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to handle your request. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary financial records. We may update this policy when the app or its services change; the effective date appears above.
