Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Nobody Workshop (“we”, “us” or “our”) develops Product Photo Resizer (the “App”). This policy describes the current development version and the third-party integrations planned or under consideration for future releases.

Your photos stay on your device for core processing. Image selection, resizing, cropping, compression, format conversion and export take place locally. We do not upload your selected photos to Nobody Workshop’s own servers. You do not need an account.

1. Photos and local access

The App accesses images you choose through your device’s image selection tools and processes them on your device. It may keep working copies or previews in local app storage and save exported images to the destination available on your device. Exported files remain until you delete them. You control photo access through Android settings and the system picker.

If you later share an exported image using another app or service, that sharing is governed by the receiving service’s privacy policy.

2. Current version and planned services

The current development version has no active Google AdMob, Google UMP, Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics integration. We plan or may integrate these services in a future release. The descriptions below apply only when the relevant service is enabled; they do not mean every service is currently collecting data.

  • Google AdMob: may process identifiers (including advertising identifiers where available), IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and app information, advertising interactions and diagnostic information to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain its services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): may be used to present consent messages and manage advertising privacy choices, including consent status and relevant device or request information.
  • Firebase Analytics: may process app interaction events, app-instance identifiers, device and app information and approximate location to help us understand usage.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: may process crash reports, stack traces, installation identifiers, device and app information and diagnostic logs to help us diagnose failures.

These services can send their own data to Google even though core photo processing remains local. We do not intend to include selected photos or image contents in advertising, analytics or crash reports. Actual collection depends on the released SDKs, configuration and your applicable privacy choices.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information and Firebase’s privacy information.

3. Consent and choices

Where required, an advertising consent message will be presented before relevant processing. When UMP is enabled and applicable, privacy options will let you review or change your choices. You can also manage advertising and app permissions through Android settings. Available choices may vary by region and release. We will update this policy and the App’s disclosures to reflect integrations actually enabled before release.

4. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information or photos unless needed for your support request. We retain communications only as needed for support, legitimate recordkeeping or applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing, retention and international processing

We do not sell your selected photos. When enabled, the Google services described above process data under their applicable policies and settings, including processing outside your country. Their retention periods depend on the service and configuration. We may disclose support information when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

6. Security and deletion

We use reasonable care to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can remove local app data through Android settings or uninstall the App. Exported images may remain in shared storage and must be deleted separately. For requests concerning support information or your applicable privacy rights, contact us below. Deleting local data does not automatically delete data previously processed by an enabled third-party service.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required.
