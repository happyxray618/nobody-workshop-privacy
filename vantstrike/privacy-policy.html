VANTSTRIKE: ARMY SHOOTER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy describes Vantstrike: Army Shooter by Nobody Workshop, Android package com.nobodyworkshop.vantstrike.

Planned offline release, version 1.0.0 (version code 4): this version contains no advertising, in-app purchases, Google Mobile Ads/UMP SDK or GameAnalytics SDK. Gameplay does not require an account or an internet connection. Advertising and analytics features tested in separate development builds are not part of this release.

Local game information

Game preferences, such as touch sensitivity, AutoShoot and weapon selection, are stored on your device. This release does not transmit these preferences or gameplay events to Nobody Workshop or to an advertising or analytics service. There is no Nobody Workshop cloud-save service. Android backup and device transfer may retain or restore local app data according to your system settings.

Advertising, analytics and permissions

This release does not request internet access, advertising ID or Android advertising-services permissions. It does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access. It does not display advertisements or request advertising or gameplay-analytics consent. If a future version adds online features, we will update the policy and provide any required notices and choices before those features operate.

External privacy page and support

The privacy-policy button opens this website in your browser. Your browser, network and website hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address, under their own practices; this is separate from offline gameplay. The page is hosted using GitHub Pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email service@gamebox.wiki, we receive your email address and the information you send, and use it to respond and investigate your request. Do not send passwords, private keys or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept as needed for the request and applicable obligations. You may ask us to delete information you provided directly, subject to those obligations.

Deletion and security

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its local data. System backup copies are controlled by Android and your settings. The app does not add separate password protection for local preferences. Your device's security controls apply; no device or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Audience

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Store content ratings may vary by region and are separate from this intended audience. Contact us with privacy concerns involving a child.

Changes and contact

We will revise this page when the game's data practices change. This policy applies only to Vantstrike: Army Shooter, not other Nobody Workshop products. It does not mean that a release has been approved or published by Google Play.
