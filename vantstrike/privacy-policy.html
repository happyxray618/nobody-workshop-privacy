VANTSTRIKE: ARMY SHOOTER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how Nobody Workshop ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in Vantstrike: Army Shooter. For privacy questions, contact service@gamebox.wiki.

Current release status: the game is in prerelease preparation. Production advertising requests and GameAnalytics gameplay-event submission are not currently enabled. The advertising sections below explain the third-party processing that can apply when advertising is enabled. We will update this notice and the relevant in-app choices before changing these practices.

1. Game data on your device

The game stores settings, such as touch sensitivity, the AutoShoot preference and weapon selection, in the app's local storage. Where an optional rewarded-ad benefit is available, its redemption record and expiry can also be stored locally to avoid awarding the same benefit twice.

The current build does not require a game account and has no Nobody Workshop cloud-save feature. We do not send these saved settings or local reward records to an advertising provider as custom advertising parameters. Android system backup or device transfer may retain or restore app data according to your device and system settings.

2. Advertising services

The game integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising-enabled versions may offer banner, interstitial, optional rewarded and app-open advertisements. The availability of an advertisement depends on privacy choices, configuration, connection and ad readiness.

Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, advertising or other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. IP addresses may indicate approximate location. These providers use information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and other purposes described in their policies. Non-personalized or limited advertising can still involve data processing.

For Google's practices and controls, see Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads data-disclosure guidance. The presence of an integrated SDK is not a promise that it processes no information before an ad is displayed.

The advertising privacy message identifies the configured advertising partners and links to their purposes and policies. This list does not mean that each partner has a separate SDK installed in the game. Some partners may request permission to use precise geolocation through a separate special-feature choice, where applicable. This does not mean that the game actually collects precise location: the current game does not request Android precise-location permission. IP addresses may indicate approximate location.

3. Privacy choices

Production advertising requires completed privacy configuration. Where applicable, UMP presents the configured advertising privacy message and determines whether ad requests are allowed. Any required privacy-options entry point must be available before advertising is enabled. The current prerelease configuration has not yet completed this activation.

You can also use the advertising ID and advertising privacy controls available in Android settings. Declining optional choices does not require you to stop playing, though advertising availability and optional rewarded offers may be affected. Changing or clearing local app choices does not automatically erase information previously processed by a third-party provider.

The app may contact Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) at startup to check applicable advertising privacy requirements, even while ad requests remain disabled. Where required, the in-game "PRIVACY OPTIONS" entry lets you reopen the configured message and change your choices. If the privacy check or form fails, this build keeps ad requests disabled. Advertising choices do not enable optional gameplay analytics.

4. Optional gameplay analytics

GameAnalytics is integrated but is not activated in the current prerelease build, and the game does not currently submit gameplay events through that service. Accepting advertising choices does not automatically enable GameAnalytics.

If we introduce optional gameplay analytics, we will update the relevant notice and provide a separate appropriate choice before enabling collection. Planned event categories concern gameplay progress and navigation, such as entering a game location, replaying, pausing or defeat. These planned categories are not a statement that collection is active. See GameAnalytics' privacy information for its service practices.

5. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Please do not send passwords, payment details, private keys or other unnecessary sensitive information. You may request deletion of information you have provided directly to us, subject to applicable retention obligations.

6. Retention and deletion

Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it removes the current local copy of its settings and reward records. Any Android backup or restored copy is governed by your system settings. We do not operate a remote game-save recovery service for this build.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations. Google and other service providers retain information they process according to their own policies and legal requirements. Deleting the game or clearing its storage does not necessarily delete third-party records; use the relevant provider's controls or contact channels.

7. Permissions and security

The current game does not request precise-location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Network access is used by online advertising and privacy functionality when those features are used, and external links may open a browser. Integrated SDKs declare advertising ID, Android advertising-service and other normal permissions in the Android manifest.

Local game information is subject to your device's security. The game does not add a separate password-protection feature for its local settings. Third-party services operate their own security controls. No storage method, device or software system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children and regional requirements

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Store content ratings are determined separately and may vary by region.

Advertising eligibility and privacy requirements can differ by country, age and user circumstances. Store age ratings and availability are determined separately from this policy. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information directly to Nobody Workshop, contact us so we can investigate and respond appropriately. This policy does not claim that the game is certified for a children's program.

9. Third parties and international processing

Google and other providers may process information in countries other than where you live under their respective policies and applicable safeguards. Advertisements and external links may lead to other websites or applications. Their content, privacy practices and security are controlled by those providers.

10. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. We will revise the date above and provide notice or choices where required.
