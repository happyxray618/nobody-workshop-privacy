VANTSTRIKE: ARMY SHOOTER

Privacy Policy

Optional usage statistics in version 1.0.1

You can enable Firebase Analytics using the usage statistics control in the app. It is off by default. When enabled, Google processes an app-instance identifier, device information, approximate location derived from the connection, session activity and events such as starting and completing levels to help Nobody Workshop understand how people use the app. You can turn it off at any time using the same control. These events exclude names, email addresses, messages and user-entered content.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy describes Vantstrike: Army Shooter by Nobody Workshop, Android package com.nobodyworkshop.vantstrike.

Version-specific notice: The current offline release, version code 8 and earlier, does not include Google Mobile Ads or UMP and does not display advertising. The following advertising and privacy-choice section applies to version code 9 and later. Gameplay does not require an account.

Advertising in version code 9 and later

Version code 9 and later include the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may collect and share device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID where available, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app and advertising interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. This information is transmitted using encryption in transit. Data use depends on device settings, privacy choices, location and the services configured. Google explains its practices at Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, the game will present privacy choices and provide an in-game Privacy Options entry so you can revisit them. You may also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising. If privacy choices cannot be checked, the game is designed to continue without requesting advertisements for that session. The game does not sell an ad-free purchase or provide in-app purchases.

Local game information

Game preferences, such as touch sensitivity, AutoShoot and weapon selection, are stored on your device. Version code 8 and earlier does not transmit these preferences or gameplay events to Nobody Workshop or to an advertising or analytics service. Version code 9 and later do not use a separate gameplay analytics SDK. There is no Nobody Workshop cloud-save service. Android backup and device transfer may retain or restore local app data according to your system settings.

Advertising, analytics and permissions

Version code 8 and earlier does not request internet access, advertising ID or Android advertising-services permissions and does not display ads. Version code 9 and later requests network access and advertising-related permissions for Google services; it does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access. The game does not use a separate gameplay analytics SDK.

External privacy page and support

The privacy-policy button opens this website in your browser. Your browser, network and website hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address, under their own practices; this is separate from offline gameplay. The page is hosted using GitHub Pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email service@gamebox.wiki, we receive your email address and the information you send, and use it to respond and investigate your request. Do not send passwords, private keys or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept as needed for the request and applicable obligations. You may ask us to delete information you provided directly, subject to those obligations.

Deletion and security

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its local data. System backup copies are controlled by Android and your settings. The app does not add separate password protection for local preferences. Your device's security controls apply; no device or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Audience

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Store content ratings may vary by region and are separate from this intended audience. Contact us with privacy concerns involving a child.

Changes and contact

We will revise this page when the game's data practices change. This policy applies only to Vantstrike: Army Shooter, not other Nobody Workshop products. It does not mean that a release has been approved or published by Google Play.
